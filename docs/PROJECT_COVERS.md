# Project cover design

Thirteen conceptual illustrations created with the built-in image generation tool for the portfolio. They are visual covers, not screenshots or measured charts. Existing dashboard evidence is kept in the project cases.

## Shared prompt

Premium editorial 3D illustration for a data analyst portfolio card. Wide 16:9 composition; dark midnight navy background; translucent glass and sculptural materials; crisp centered focal object; cinematic rim light. Readable at thumbnail size. No project titles, logos, watermarks, UI panels or factual charts.

## Subjects

- **ConversaAI** (`assets/project-covers/conversaai.webp`): Glowing conversational speech bubbles orbiting a sophisticated language-processing neural core, flowing multilingual abstract glyphs, cyan and violet glass, no readable text.
- **TechCore · Ventas** (`assets/project-covers/techcore.webp`): Modern retail point of sale and shopping bag connected to luminous business intelligence cubes and rising abstract bars, amber and cyan.
- **Bank Transactions** (`assets/project-covers/bank.webp`): Elegant digital banking transaction network, brushed metal bank vault ring and streams of luminous teal transaction tokens, blue and emerald.
- **Churn Intelligence** (`assets/project-covers/churn.webp`): Customer retention concept, orbiting luminous customer nodes with one coral node drifting away and a protective teal network reconnecting it, violet and emerald.
- **EquineLead** (`assets/project-covers/equinelead.webp`): Beautiful sculptural horse head in dark chrome with turquoise edge light, subtle data constellation flowing around it, luxurious equestrian data intelligence.
- **LatAm Insights** (`assets/project-covers/market.webp`): Stylized Latin American continent formed from glowing dots, connected professional opportunity cards and magnifying glass, blue and warm orange.
- **FleetLogix** (`assets/project-covers/fleetlogix.webp`): Miniature logistics delivery truck and warehouse with luminous route network winding across a dark map surface, teal and amber.
- **Cliente360** (`assets/project-covers/cliente360.webp`): Customer segmentation, three clusters of translucent colorful human silhouette tokens circling a central 360 degree analytical ring, cyan violet and coral.
- **Riesgo crediticio** (`assets/project-covers/credit.webp`): Credit risk concept with polished shield and layered financial data cards, balanced scales and translucent confidence bands, blue and amber.
- **Analytics Toolkit** (`assets/project-covers/toolkit.webp`): Modular data analytics toolkit, interlocking glass data cubes, code brackets and a small analytical lens, cyan purple and orange.
- **KrioMetrics** (`assets/project-covers/kriometrics.webp`): Refrigerant thermodynamics, sculptural cooling coil with icy frost crystals, cyan vapor and a contrasting warm copper flow, blue teal and orange.
- **Simpson 1/3** (`assets/project-covers/simpson.webp`): Numerical integration illustration with elegant luminous curved ribbon above a sequence of translucent area blocks on a mathematical plane, purple and gold, no numbers or exact chart.
- **HealthPredict y FinanceGuard** (`assets/project-covers/m4.webp`): Two connected analytical worlds: translucent medical cross and heartbeat ribbon on one side and financial shield and customer nodes on the other, teal blue and violet.

## Presentation

All cards and the featured section use locally hosted landscape WebP covers. Henry projects are ordered 01–05; No Country projects are ordered 01–04 chronologically; personal projects are ordered 01–04. Module 4 retains both HealthPredict and FinanceGuard within one card.
